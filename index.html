I am Assa Nsabiyeze, an agricultural engineer and doctoral researcher specialising in precision livestock farming (PLF), multimodal sensing, artificial intelligence, animal behaviour and welfare, and sustainable livestock systems. My research combines wearable and IoT sensing, machine learning and deep learning, causal-temporal modelling, life-cycle assessment (LCA), data envelopment analysis (DEA), input-output analysis, and environmental assessment to develop data-driven approaches for livestock monitoring and low-carbon agricultural systems.

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Agricultural Engineering at China Agricultural University in Beijing, China, under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Xiaoshuan Zhang and Associate Prof. Dr. Mengjie Zhang. My doctoral work develops interpretable causal-temporal graph learning methods for multimodal sheep behaviour and welfare recognition, integrating wearable sensors, physiological measurements, behavioural observations, and explainable AI.

Before my PhD, I completed a Master of Engineering in Agricultural Engineering at China Agricultural University in 2024, where my research focused on the productivity and environmental performance of sheep farming using DEA, LCA, and predictive modelling. I also completed Advanced Chinese Language Studies at Bohai University in Jinzhou, China. I obtained my BSc (Honours) in Agronomy and Bio-Engineering from the University of Burundi in Bujumbura, Burundi, under the supervision of Dr. Jean Ndimubandi and Prof. Dr. Sanctus Niragira.
