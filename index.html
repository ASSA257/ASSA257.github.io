I am Assa Nsabiyeze, an agricultural engineer and doctoral researcher specialising in precision livestock farming, multimodal sensing, artificial intelligence, animal behaviour and welfare, and sustainable livestock systems. My research integrates wearable and IoT sensing, machine learning and deep learning, causal-temporal modelling, life-cycle assessment (LCA), data envelopment analysis (DEA), input-output analysis, and environmental assessment.

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Agricultural Engineering at China Agricultural University in Beijing under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Xiaoshuan Zhang and Associate Prof. Dr. Mengjie Zhang. My doctoral work develops interpretable causal-temporal graph learning methods for multimodal sheep behaviour and welfare recognition.

Before my PhD, I completed a Master of Engineering in Agricultural Engineering at China Agricultural University, Advanced Chinese Language Studies at Bohai University, and a BSc (Honours) in Agronomy and Bio-Engineering at the University of Burundi, where I was supervised by Dr. Jean Ndimubandi and Prof. Dr. Sanctus Niragira.
